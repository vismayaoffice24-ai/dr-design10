Our services

Complete nephrology care, from first diagnosis to long-term follow-up.

Chronic Kidney Disease Management

Diagnosis, staging and long-term care with diet and medicine plans to slow progression and protect the kidney function you have.

Acute Kidney Injury

Fast assessment and treatment when kidney function drops suddenly.

Dialysis Guidance

Planning, access care and monitoring for patients on dialysis.

Transplant Follow-up

Regular care and monitoring after a kidney transplant.

Diabetic Kidney Care

Early screening and treatment to protect kidneys in diabetes.

Hypertension & Kidney Care

Blood pressure control to protect the kidneys.

Kidney Stone Evaluation

Finding the cause of stones and advice to prevent them.

Protein or Blood in Urine

Tests to find why urine reports are abnormal.

Kidney Check-ups

Kidney function tests for people with diabetes, high BP or family history.
